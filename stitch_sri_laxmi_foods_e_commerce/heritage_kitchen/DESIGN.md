## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, craftsmanship, and trust of traditional Indian home cooking, elevated for modern culinary commerce. The experience balances nostalgic artisanal authenticity with frictionless, contemporary shopping ergonomics.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
The visual philosophy pairs **Warm Artisanal Heritage** with **Understated Modern Editorial** styling:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Surfaces reject clinical pure gray tones in favor of sun-dried ivory, hand-ground spice tints, and natural clays.
- **Tactile Softness:** Gentle border radii, pill counters, and organic card structures invoke pantry jars, hand-tied parcels, and kitchen brassware without resorting to heavy skeuomorphism.
- **Editorial Legibility:** Literary serifs establish lineage and authority in hero narratives, counterbalanced by clean, utilitarian grotesques for nutritional disclosures, ingredient lists, weights, and transactional tables.

### Emotional Targets
- **Nostalgic & Reassuring:** Evokes generational kitchen memories, purity, and mother's recipes.
- **Wholesome & Pure:** Feels pesticide-free, preservative-free, and meticulously prepared.
- **Culinary Elegance:** Premium without becoming aloof; approachable luxury rooted in South Asian pantry traditions.

## Colors

The palette is derived from indigenous Indian spices, brass utensils, sun-ripened crops, and polished teak leaves.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1F4D3A` - Deep Forest Green):** The anchor of reliability, natural harvest, and brand distinction. Applied to key interactive triggers, navigation anchors, sticky bars, and active state indicators.
- **Secondary (`#C8631F` - Earthy Orange):** Evokes roasted chili, jaggery, and red clay pottery. Deployed for secondary call-to-actions, offer tags, limited-batch announcements, and hover highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D9A21B` - Turmeric Yellow):** Symbolizes auspiciousness, freshly bloomed mustard, and ground turmeric. Reserved for badges, rating stars, artisanal awards, and special festive highlights.
- **Neutral Core (`#5A3E2B` - Deep Natural Brown):** Serves as primary text typography instead of sterile carbon black. Ensures natural contrast against ivory backgrounds while maintaining editorial warmth.

### Functional & Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBF6EA` (Ivory Canvas). Generates an inviting, ambient backdrop.
- **Container / Section Fill:** `#F5EBD7` (Warm Cream). Delineates sub-sections, ingredient breakdowns, and secondary shelves.
- **Card Elevated Fill:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White). Isolates product imagery, nutrition cards, and forms for maximum contrast and hygiene.
- **Muted Text / Metadata:** `#7C5E48` (Sun-baked Teak). Supporting captions, timestamps, SKU metadata, and secondary pricing labels.
- **System Destructive / Warning:** `#B3392F` (Soft Kashmiri Red). Form error boundaries, out-of-stock chips, and transactional alerts.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dual rhythm:
1. **The Display Voice (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the lyrical storytelling of slow-crafted pickles, sun-dried spices, and family heritage. Used for hero phrases, catalog section titles, collection heads, and artisan pull-quotes.
2. **The Operational Voice (`DM Sans`):** Ensures flawless clarity for package weights, batch dates, ingredient allergens, cart totals, and interactive buttons. Its geometry brings contemporary digital precision to the organic packaging aesthetic.

### Letter-Spacing Rules
- All `label-sm` and uppercase badges carry tracking of `+0.04em` to preserve clarity against colored backgrounds.
- Large editorial display headlines (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`) feature subtle negative tracking (`-0.015em`) for dense, stately balance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout balances disciplined e-commerce utility with spacious, unhurried pacing.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid. 1200px max content bounding container with centered auto-margins, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) page margin boundaries.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid, 20px gutters, 24px margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) margins.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`):** Internal component binding—gap between an icon and button text, badge padding, spice heat indicator dots.
- **Component Flow (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Product card interior gutters, form field vertical stacks, ingredient card pairings.
- **Section Breaks (`space-xl` and multiples):** Generous vertical pauses between storytelling blocks, curation carousels, and footer areas, avoiding density that could make the home-food experience feel commercialized.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic neon dropshadows or heavy black drop-casts, opting for warm, diffused ambient shadows infused with natural brown and amber undertones.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Flat Base (Canvas & Structural Containers):** The ivory background (`#FBF6EA`) sits at elevation 0 with no border or shadow.
2. **Surface Layer (Cards, Trays, Modal Wrappers):** White cards (`#FFFFFF`) feature a warm, ambient halo:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.04)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.08)` to prevent edge bleed on creamy containers.
3. **Interactive Lift (Product Hover & Selectables):**
   - `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.06)`
   - Transform: Subtle vertical translate of `-2px`.
4. **Floating Overlays (Cart Drawer, Sticky Checkout Bar, Quick-View Modal):**
   - `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -6px rgba(31, 77, 58, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.08)`
   - Soft backdrop tint: `rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy establishes clear distinction between structured content containers and interactive or contextual elements:

- **Cards & Inputs (12px / `rounded-lg`):** Product display containers, nutrition panels, quantity selectors, checkout fields, and review cards use an exact 12px curvature. This provides a soft, welcoming feel while maintaining rigid space efficiency.
- **Badges, Chips & Buttons (999px / Full Pill):** Filter pills, spice-level indicators, weight variation selectors (e.g., "250g", "500g", "1kg"), and primary action triggers utilize full pill curves. This shape evokes hand-pressed snacks and handcrafted jar seals.
- **Visual Media Containers:** Product imagery uses matching 12px border radius, occasionally accompanied by subtle continuous border outlines to resemble framed pantry prints.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled `#1F4D3A` (Deep Forest Green) with white text, pill radius (`999px`), bold body label, 12px vertical by 24px horizontal padding. Hover state transitions to `#16392B` with a subtle elevation lift.
- **Secondary / Action Button:** `#C8631F` (Earthy Orange) fill or outline with `#5A3E2B` typography. Used for secondary triggers like "Add to Cart" inline with "Buy Now".
- **Ghost / Narrative Button:** Text-only in `#1F4D3A` with an underlined hover state, accompanied by a trailing organic arrow icon (`->`).

### Chips & Weight Switchers
- **Weight Selectors (`250g`, `500g`, `1kg`):** Pill-shaped with a 1px border of `rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.2)`. Background is transparent ivory. On selection, fills with `#1F4D3A` and switches text to pure white.
- **Badges & Status Tags:** Pill-shaped with 4px vertical by 10px horizontal padding. 
  - *Bestseller / Authentic:* `#D9A21B` background with `#5A3E2B` bold text.
  - *Seasonal / Limited:* `#C8631F` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Product Cards
- Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- Corner Radius: 12px.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.08)`.
- Image Frame: Aspect ratio 1:1 with soft beige background padding (`#F5EBD7`), showcasing the artisanal textures of sweets, masalas, or snacks.
- Information Order: Category/Origin subtitle (`label-sm`), Product Name in `headline-sm`, Weight selector chip row, Price tag with strike-through MRP, and a full-width or icon-assisted Primary Pill Button.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Corner Radius: 12px.
- Background: `#FFFFFF`.
- Border: `1.5px solid rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.2)`.
- Text color: `#5A3E2B` with placeholder in `rgba(124, 94, 72, 0.6)`.
- Focus State: Border color switches to `#1F4D3A` with an ambient glow of `rgba(31, 77, 58, 0.15)`.
- Error State: Border switches to `#B3392F` with assistive micro-copy beneath in soft red.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkbox: 4px rounded corners; Radio: 100% circle.
- Inactive: 1.5px border in `rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.3)`.
- Checked: Filled with `#1F4D3A` featuring a crisp white SVG checkmark or dot.

### Special Domain Components
- **Spice Intensity Meter:** A sequential row of 3 to 5 miniature clay-red chili or dot glyphs displaying mild, medium, or sharp pungency.
- **Heritage Story Accordion:** Embedded on product detail sheets; contains accordion headers styled in `headline-sm` with delicate divider lines in `rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.12)` revealing shelf-life, storage instructions, and traditional culinary pairings.

Our Story: Rooted in Tradition, Made with Love

Sri Laxmi Home Foods started from a humble family kitchen to bring the nostalgic flavors of home-cooked snacks and traditional pantry staples to households across India.

What began as sharing lovingly prepared parcels of festive sweets, sundried crisps, and spiced pickles with extended relatives soon evolved into a culinary calling. We honor the ancestral recipes passed through handwritten kitchen diaries, grinding whole spices and simmering preserves with patience, warmth, and uncompromised respect for South Asian traditions.

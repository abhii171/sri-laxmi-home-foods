The Laxmi Standard

Stone-Pounded, Not Heat-Pulverized

Commercial high-speed steel mills burn away delicate volatile oils during grinding. In our family kitchen, whole spices are roasted in heavy iron kadhais and milled at deliberate slow speeds to lock in authentic pungency and traditional kitchen warmth.

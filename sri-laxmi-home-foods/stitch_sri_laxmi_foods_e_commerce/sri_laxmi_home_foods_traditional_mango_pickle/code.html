Artisanal Specification

Culinary Heritage & Ingredients

Our traditional Mango Pickle (Avakaya) is rooted in age-old coastal Andhra culinary methods. Raw, firm, sour green mangoes are carefully sourced from trusted local orchards, washed, dried with cotton cloths, and hand-cut into uniform bite-sized pieces with a portion of the hard inner shell preserved to maintain crunch during aging.

The cut pieces are layered with sun-dried Guntur chili powder, stone-ground mustard seeds (aava pindi), pure crystal salt, and toasted fenugreek. Cured in glazed ceramic jars (jaadis) under natural room temperatures with pure cold-pressed sesame and mustard oil, the pickle matures gracefully, developing its signature robust tang and deep aromatic pungency.

Raw Mango Chunks 62% by weight
Cold-Pressed Oils 18% (Sesame & Mustard)
Pure Spices Guntur Chilli, Mustard, Hing
Natural Seasoning Iodised Salt & Fenugreek
